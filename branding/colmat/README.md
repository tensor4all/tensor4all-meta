Status: proposal, not yet adopted. colmat is the new name of the tensor4all faer fork, published until now as `t4a-faer`.

## Concept

The **m** of colmat is drawn as the entries of a matrix. They form two peaks, and the entry between them, in the accent color, is the *col*: French for a mountain pass, the saddle point between two summits.

There are two layouts and two palettes, all generated from the same pattern:

| Layout | Files | Shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plain | `colmat-wordmark*.svg` | Only the nonzero entries |
| Grid | `colmat-wordmark-grid*.svg` | The full 5 by 13 matrix, zeros in a pale tone |

| Palette | Files | Colors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Family | no suffix | Ink and copper, shared with tensor4all and tenferro |
| Fresh | `-fresh` | The original navy and amber |

Favicons use a smaller matrix (4 by 7, and 3 by 5 at 16 px) with the same two peaks and col.

## Colors

| The col | `#a3521a` | `#f0a46b` | `#dca240` | `#e8b24f` |
| Zeros (grid layout) | `#d6d2c8` | `#3a414a` | `#d3d6d8` | `#3a4450` |
| Avatar background | `#fbfaf7` | `#14171b` | `#f7f6f3` | `#14171b` |

The wordmark is set in IBM Plex Sans SemiBold (SIL Open Font License 1.1) with tracking of -0.03 em and kerning, converted to outlines, like every tensor4all logo. The matrix m spans the x-height, from the baseline to the top of the lowercase letters.
