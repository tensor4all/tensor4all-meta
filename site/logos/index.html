Branding

tensor4all logos

Logos of the tensor4all project and its libraries, with the files to download, their colors, and how to put them in a README. The sources live under branding/ in tensor4all-meta.

Every logo on this page is a proposal. None has been adopted yet, so expect changes before you put one on a release.

One family

The logos share one typeface and one palette, and are generated by one script, so they stay consistent when one of them changes.

ElementRule
TypeIBM Plex Sans SemiBold (SIL Open Font License 1.1), tracking -0.03 em, kerning on, converted to outlines
Ink#1d2126 on light, #e6e8eb on dark
Copper accent#a3521a on light, #f0a46b on dark
ProportionsIn a horizontal wordmark the x-height is 0.47 of the mark height, and the gap between mark and text is 0.2 of it. The tensor4all mark is itself lettering, so its text sits on the mark's baseline with an x-height of 0.45 of the T4∀ cap height

tensor4all

Proposal

The organization logo: T4∀, “tensor for all”, drawn as tensor diagrams of bonds and solid nodes. T is a three-leg tensor, the 4 a small network and ∀ a triangle of three tensors. As in Jan’s earlier circuit mark, the strokes mimic the letter t, numeral 4 and letter a in the name, and the copper color used for 4 is the same as for the 4 in the wordmark. Idea by Xavier. Favicons keep only the copper 4.

tenferro

Proposal

The logo of tenferro-rs, the tensor runtime. The mark is a hex nut with a tensor-product sign as its hole. Ferro is iron, and iron rusts: the nut and the copper say that tenferro is written in Rust. The tensor product stands for general tensor computation rather than any one method.

colmat

Proposal

The logo of colmat, the tensor4all fork of faer, published until now as t4a-faer. The m is drawn as the entries of a matrix that form two peaks. The entry between them, in the accent color, is the col: French for a mountain pass, the saddle point between two summits.

It comes in the family palette and in its original fresh navy and amber, each with or without the zero entries shown.

Putting a logo in a README

Adding a logo

  1. Add the project's geometry and an output section to branding/tools/export_logos.py, using the shared type and palette.
  2. Run it to write branding/<project>/svg/ and png/, then add a README.md there, following branding/tenferro/.
  3. Add a section for the project to this page, site/logos/index.html. The Pages workflow copies svg/ and png/ from every folder under branding/ into the site.
Maintained in tensor4all-meta. Corrections are welcome as issues or pull requests there.
