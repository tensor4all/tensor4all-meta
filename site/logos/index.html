Branding

tensor4all logos

Logos of the tensor4all project and its libraries, with the files to download, their colors, and how to put them in a README. The sources live under branding/ in tensor4all-meta.

Every logo on this page is a proposal. None has been adopted yet, so expect changes before you put one on a release.

tensor4all

Proposal

The organization logo. The mark is a tesseract, a four-dimensional hypercube drawn as a cube inside a cube. A rank-4 tensor carries the 4 in the name, and the mark shows a tensor itself rather than a network diagram.

tenferro

Proposal

The logo of tenferro-rs, the tensor runtime. The mark is a hex nut with a tensor-product sign as its hole. Ferro is iron, and iron rusts: the nut and the copper-rust color say that tenferro is written in Rust. The tensor product stands for general tensor computation rather than any one method.

Adding a logo

  1. Put the files under branding/<project>/ with svg/, png/, tools/ and a README.md, following branding/tenferro/.
  2. Outline the text in every wordmark, so it renders without the font installed.
  3. Add a section for the project to this page, site/logos/index.html. The Pages workflow copies svg/ and png/ from every folder under branding/ into the site.
Maintained in tensor4all-meta. Corrections are welcome as issues or pull requests there.
